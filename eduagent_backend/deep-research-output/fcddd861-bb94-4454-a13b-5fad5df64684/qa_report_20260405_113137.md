

## Audit Summary

The report is generally trustworthy and well-constructed, with careful hedging about the limits of direct evidence. The bibliography is well-linked to inline citations, and most statistics are traceable to the iteration history and source list findings. The most critical issues are: (1) a factual error in the executive summary where the report states LearnLM students were "5.5 percentage points more likely than those with human tutors" to answer correctly, but the iteration history (Iteration 1) states the opposite direction — "students receiving human tutoring were 5.5 percentage points more likely than LearnLM-supported students"; however, the source list finding itself states LearnLM was +0.055 over human tutors, so the report's direction matches the source list but contradicts one iteration summary; (2) the source [2] has no URL in the pre-numbered source list ("not available"), yet the bibliography also omits a URL, which is technically consistent but worth noting; (3) one statistic from [46] (effect size 0.70 for Task 7) is verified against the source list but the composite 0.475 is described in the report as a "composite mathematics performance effect" which matches the source. Overall, the report is careful, accurate in most respects, and appropriately cautious.
