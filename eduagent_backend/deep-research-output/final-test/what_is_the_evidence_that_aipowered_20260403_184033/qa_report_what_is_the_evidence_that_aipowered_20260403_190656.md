

## Audit Summary

The report is generally trustworthy and well-constructed, with most claims traceable to the source pool and iteration history. The bibliography is complete and URLs are accurate for academic-DB sources. The most critical issues are: (1) several inline citations reference studies using parenthetical author-date format (e.g., "(Kim, 2015)", "(Belland, 2017)", "(Belland, 2018)", "(AI Knows Best, 2026)") that do not appear in the numbered bibliography table, creating linkage gaps; (2) a few statistics are unverifiable against the source pool summaries because exact values are not present in the provided findings; and (3) the PhET study [35] is flagged by the report itself as potentially mislabeled as an RCT when the summary describes a one-group pretest-posttest design. Overall, the report is careful, appropriately hedged, and largely accurate, but the non-numbered citations represent a significant structural problem.
