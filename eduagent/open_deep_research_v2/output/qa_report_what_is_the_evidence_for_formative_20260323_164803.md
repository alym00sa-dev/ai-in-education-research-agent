This is the single most severe issue and would normally render the report non-publishable. Beyond this, the statistics cited are mostly verifiable from the iteration history, study design labels are reasonable given the source material, and sub-question coverage is addressed (though unevenly). URL integrity cannot be checked against bibliography entries since none exist, but can be assessed against the PreScoredTiers. The report's substantive conclusions are defensible, but the missing bibliography is a fundamental flaw.
