

## Audit Summary

The report is moderately trustworthy but contains several issues that reduce confidence. The most critical problems are: (1) a mislabeled study ([193], which the report cites as a large-scale RCT in 140 English secondary schools, but the supplementary source describes a study on students with intellectual disabilities, not the Anders et al. 2022 EFA trial); (2) several statistics and claims attributed to sources that cannot be verified against the iteration history or source list; (3) one orphan bibliography entry ([238]) that has no inline citation in the report body; and (4) some conflation of source identities where the bibliography entry for [193] does not match the study described in the report text. The URL integrity is generally sound, with all notes-sourced URLs matching the supplementary list and no academic-DB URL issues. Sub-question coverage is adequate for Tiers 1 and 4 but thin for Tiers 2 and 3.
