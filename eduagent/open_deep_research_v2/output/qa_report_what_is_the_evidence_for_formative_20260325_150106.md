The report is generally trustworthy in its narrative claims and appropriately cautious in its confidence assessments. The bibliography aligns well with the pre-numbered source list and supplementary sources, and most statistics are traceable to the iteration history. However, there are several issues: one inline citation ([321]) references a source whose connection to the report's claims is only loosely supported by the iteration history; two inline citations reference sources without formal bibliography entries (Lichtenberger et al. and Matilda & Helen are discussed inline but not given numbered bibliography entries); the report cites source [315] (van den Ham & Heinze, 2022) inline without a bibliography row with that number appearing in the bibliography table; and a few statistics could not be verified to exact wording in the iteration history. Study design labels cannot be fully verified for notes-sourced papers since the bibliography lists them all as "not_reported," but the iteration history descriptions are broadly consistent with the labels used in the report text. URLs for academic-DB sources match well, and supplementary source URLs are plausible.

## Check 3 — Study Design Accuracy

For all bibliography entries, the bibliography lists "not_reported" as the design, which is expected for notes-sourced papers ([182]+). Checking the report's labels against iteration history:

1. **[185] Xuan et al. (2022)** — Report calls it "meta-analysis." Iteration history confirms "meta-analyzed 48 studies." The pre-numbered source list [185] does not exist in [1]–[181] (it's supplementary). Supplementary source confirmed as meta-analysis. **OK.**
2. **[214] Sortwell et al. (2024)** — Report calls it "umbrella review" of 13 meta-analyses. Iteration history confirms. **OK.**
3. **[243] Karaman (2021)** — Report calls it "meta-analysis." Iteration history confirms "meta-analyzed 32 studies." **OK.**
4. **[186] Gezer et al. (2021)** — Report calls it "quasi-experimental." The pre-numbered source [100] (same paper) is listed as "Observational / Correlational" in the pre-numbered list. However, the report says "quasi-experimental study in primary-grade mathematics." The iteration history says "positive association between formative and summative assessment data" — which sounds correlational/observational. **POTENTIAL MISLABEL**: The pre-numbered source list entry [100] labels this as "Observational / Correlational," but the report calls it "quasi-experimental." This is a mislabelling.
5. **[184] Ismail et al. (2022)** — Report references it for motivation/self-regulation findings. The pre-numbered source [102] labels it as "Quasi-Experimental Design (QED)." The report does not explicitly label [184]'s design in the body but references it among observational/review evidence in higher education. **OK** (no explicit design claim in report body that contradicts).
6. **Lichtenberger et al.** — Report calls it "cluster-randomized controlled intervention" and "cluster-randomized physics intervention." Iteration history (iteration 3) says "In an RCT in secondary physics, Lichtenberger et al." — consistent with RCT label. Cannot verify against pre-numbered or supplementary lists since no bibliography entry exists. Based on iteration history: **OK.**
7. **Matilda & Helen** — Report calls it "randomized study." Iteration history says "pre-test/post-test experimental design with 80 students." The supplementary source [273] exists. The iteration history does not clearly confirm randomization, only "experimental design." **UNVERIFIABLE** but not clearly wrong.
8. **[221] Beekman et al. (2021)** — Report calls it "longitudinal study." Pre-numbered source [154] (same paper) is labeled "Quasi-Experimental Design (QED)." The report's "longitudinal study" label understates the QED design. **MINOR MISLABEL**: Should be identified as QED per [154].

**Issues found:**
- [186] Gezer et al.: labeled "quasi-experimental" in report but "Observational / Correlational" in pre-numbered source [100].
- [221] Beekman et al.: labeled "longitudinal study" in report but "Quasi-Experimental Design (QED)" in pre-numbered source [154]. This is a conservative mislabel (understating, not overstating).
