

## Audit Summary

The report is broadly trustworthy in its narrative claims and directional conclusions, with most statistics traceable to the iteration history and bibliography entries generally matching the pre-scored source list. However, there are several notable issues: (1) the report cites source [345] inline and in the bibliography, but [345] does not appear in the pre-numbered source list (PreScoredTiers), making its provenance unverifiable through the provided materials; (2) the report describes study [168] as a "large cluster RCT" and study [249] as a "pre-test/post-test experimental design," but the PreScoredTiers labels both as "not_reported" for study design, and [168]'s PreScoredTiers entry is a separate EEF evaluation report [101] — raising questions about whether [168] and [101] are the same study cited under different source numbers; (3) several statistics from [218] (the Gezer et al. HLM study) include a coefficient (b = -0.005, p = .01 for classroom mean moderation on hiding) that partially conflicts with the iteration history, which reports b = -0.002, p = .94 for a different moderator; (4) a few sub-questions are addressed only with thin or indirect evidence. Overall, the report is a reasonable synthesis but requires corrections to design labels, one potentially fabricated statistic, and verification of source [345].
